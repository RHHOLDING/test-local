FAQ

Questions — answered briefly

What exactly is included in the fixed installation price?

Everything listed in the installation package: travel within the service area, mounting of indoor and outdoor unit, up to 3 m of line set and cable, one wall opening, standard materials, commissioning, function test and handover. You confirm the scope before you order.

What happens if my installation needs more work?

Your installer documents the extra work in our partner portal with photos and a price. You receive the request and decide — work only continues after your approval, and the approved amount is added to your Hantech invoice. No surprises on site.

Can I buy without installation?

Yes. Choose “Without installation” and your system is shipped to you. Note that split systems must be filled and commissioned by a certified refrigerant technician.

Where do you install?

We are building a nationwide partner network across Germany. Enter your postcode at checkout to see if installation is available in your area — more European countries will follow.

How efficient are HANTECH air conditioners?

Many models reach energy class A++ or A+++. Most also work as heat pumps and can heat efficiently in the transitional season.
